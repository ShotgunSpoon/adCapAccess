AdVenture Capitalist Access
Gameplay and keyboard reference

AdVenture Capitalist Access adds keyboard navigation and NVDA speech to the
Windows Steam version of AdVenture Capitalist. Keep NVDA running and the game
window focused while using the commands below.

THE GAMEPLAY LOOP

Buy a business, run its production, and spend the earnings on more businesses.
Each business has its own price, payout, and production time. Buying more of an
investment increases its income. Hiring its manager keeps production running
without having to start each cycle yourself.

Upgrades improve production. Unlocks award bonuses when you reach the listed
requirements, such as owning a certain number of an investment. Check the
requirement and the reward separately: the number in a reward description may
be the size of the bonus rather than the amount you need to own.

As a planet earns money, Angel Investors become available. Claiming them restarts
that planet's businesses and adds a profit bonus for the next run. The Investors
screen reads the Angels you own, the amount available to claim, and the bonus.
Some purchases cost Angels, so read the currency before activating a purchase.

The usual cycle is to expand your businesses, buy useful upgrades, reach unlocks,
then claim Angels when you decide the added bonus is worth restarting. Earth,
Moon, and Mars have their own businesses and progression. AdVentures contains
planet travel and access requirements.

KEYBOARD COMMANDS

Left / Right Arrow
  Move between businesses on the planet screen, or between items in a menu.

Up / Down Arrow
  Move through the selected business or item's information and actions.

Enter / Space
  Activate the selected action. On a business purchase row, hold either key to
  buy repeatedly while you can afford the purchase. Release the key to stop.

Left Bracket / Right Bracket: [ and ]
  Move backward or forward through the main screens. While the event interface
  is open, move through its Details, Goals, Rewards, and Leaderboard sections.

F6
  Repeat the current business or menu item.

F7
  Read the current status in a menu or dialog, or in the event interface.

F8
  Enter screen-text mode. Up and Down Arrow read the visible text. Enter, Space,
  Escape, or F8 returns to control navigation. Leaving this mode with Enter or
  Space does not activate the text you were reading.

C
  Read cash, Gold, and Mega Bucks.

E
  From Earth, Moon, or Mars, enter the active event. On an event planet, open or
  close the accessible event interface. If no event is active, the mod says so.

Shift+E
  Save and leave the event planet, returning to the last normal planet visited.

T
  Read the time until the active event ends. If none is active, read the time
  until the next scheduled event starts, when that information is available.

G
  Enter or leave Mega Ticket gilding mode on Earth, Moon, or Mars.

W
  Read how many Time Warp Expresses are available on the current screen.

Shift+W
  Use one available Time Warp Express on the current planet. This spends the
  item immediately; it is not a command to inspect it.

Home / End
  Raise or lower the music volume by 10 percent.

Escape
  Leave gilding or screen-text mode. Elsewhere, the game's normal Escape action
  handles menus and dialogs; a screen's Close action is also available when shown.

BUSINESSES AND MENU NAVIGATION

On the business screen, Left and Right choose an investment. Up and Down move
through its details, production action, and purchase action. An investment you
do not own presents its initial purchase. For an owned business, the mod reads
information such as quantity owned, earnings, production time, and purchase cost.
An automated business does not need a manual production start.

In menus, Left and Right choose an item. Up and Down move between its name,
description, price or progress, and action. Read the action before pressing Enter
or Space. Selecting an information row does not mean you have selected Buy,
Claim, Equip, or another action.

Managers and Upgrades can contain more entries than fit on screen. Continue
through the list with the arrow keys; the mod follows the items as the game
scrolls. Managers announces the first and last manager. Section selectors such
as Cash and Angels are separate controls, and an Angels section may have no
eligible purchases on the current planet.

MAIN SCREENS

The bracket keys cycle through these screens in this order:
Businesses, Managers, Upgrades, Unlocks, Investors, Shop, Career, AdVentures,
and Connect.

Businesses
  The current planet's investments, production, income, and purchases.

Managers
  Hire managers to automate businesses. Read the cost and currency; some
  sections use cash and others use Angels.

Upgrades
  Purchasable bonuses. Read which investment or group is affected, what the
  bonus does, and what it costs.

Unlocks
  Milestones and their bonuses. These unlock automatically when their
  requirements are met; they are different from upgrades you buy.

Investors
  Owned and pending Angels, the profit bonus, and the claim-and-restart action.
  Read the confirmation before completing a restart.

Shop
  Offers, boosts, currencies, and purchases. Check both the price and the
  currency, including whether an offer uses real money.

Career
  Character and inventory information, including cosmetics and equipment
  actions when those items are available.

AdVentures
  Planet choices, travel actions, and information about locked destinations.

Connect
  Social links and related controls or rewards provided by the game.

Other screens and dialogs
  The mod also reads Gallery categories and unlock details, Statistics,
  Exchange, Tickets, News, Welcome Back earnings, reward celebrations, and
  confirmation dialogs. Open these through their available menu controls.
  Use the same item-and-action navigation, or F8 to inspect visible text.

EVENTS

Events are temporary planets with their own investments, scoring, goals, and
rewards. Press E from a normal planet to travel to the active event. If an
introduction appears, choose its Continue action. Once the event has loaded,
press E to open the accessible event interface.

Use [ and ] for the four event sections. Left and Right move between items;
Up and Down read each item's fields and actions.

Details
  The event name, remaining time, scoring rules, score, and available rank.
  Read "How this event works" because events do not all score the same way.

Goals
  Goal requirements, progress, and available actions. In goal-based events,
  completing a goal and claiming it are separate steps. Select its Claim
  action and press Enter or Space to add the points.

Rewards
  Milestone progress, milestone rewards, and leaderboard reward information.
  Claim an available milestone through its action. A hidden reward stays a
  mystery until the game reveals it.

Leaderboard
  Event scores and standings. Use the available refresh action to request
  standings again. If rank information has not arrived, the mod says it is
  unavailable rather than assigning a rank.

In Angel-based events, claiming Angels increases the event score. Milestone
rewards can be claimed during the event; final leaderboard rewards come after
it ends. Follow the scoring explanation in Details for the current event.

Press E to leave the event interface and return to business navigation on that
event planet. Shift+E travels back to the last normal planet. The event interface
closes if the event changes, ends, or another planet begins loading.

MEGA TICKETS AND TIME WARPS

Press G on a normal planet to list owned investments that can use a Mega Ticket.
The mod reads the ticket count and the selected investment. Choose with the
arrow keys, then press Enter or Space to open the game's confirmation dialog.
G or Escape leaves the selection without choosing an investment.

Temporary events use a separate Gold-based gilding system. The G menu is for
Mega Tickets on Earth, Moon, and Mars.

W reads the Time Warp Express count when that control is available. Shift+W
uses one immediately. The mod reports when the item is unavailable or none
remain.

USING THE PATCHER AND SAVING THIS GUIDE

Close the game before using Install or Update, Repair, or Restore Original Game.
Check that the patcher points to the correct game folder, then launch the game
through Steam after the operation finishes. Repair reinstalls the mod files.
Restore Original Game uses the patcher's original assembly backup.

In patchers with the README controls, a successful installation offers to save
this guide. Download README opens a Save As dialog so you can choose the folder
and filename. You can use that button when the mod is already up to date and
without installing or repairing the game. If the download fails, the patcher
offers to save its included copy instead.

Project and patcher downloads:
https://github.com/ShotgunSpoon/adCapAccess

PLAYING AND REPORTING PROBLEMS

Availability depends on the current planet, unlocked features, and event. Some
late-game equipment, planet completion, event variations, and reward flows still
need more testing. An empty section does not always mean navigation has failed.

For a navigation problem, include the planet or event, the screen, the keys you
pressed, the words NVDA announced, and what you expected to happen. F6 repeats
the current selection; F8 can help identify other text on the screen.
